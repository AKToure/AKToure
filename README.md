### About

I am the **Founder and Chairman of Noether Meridian Inc.**, a Canadian company working at the intersection of quantitative finance, scientific research and financial technology. I also co-founded **Alpha Stochastic Research (ASR)**, its quantitative research division.

My interests centre on mathematical and computational finance: stochastic processes, interest-rate and derivatives modelling, numerical methods, portfolio risk, and the design of reproducible quantitative research systems.

I am particularly interested in the chain from **mathematical assumptions → computational implementation → independent validation → research evidence**. My goal is to help make complex quantitative models understandable, testable and useful under real-world constraints.

### Selected research

- **[Bachelier's Theory of Speculation Revisited](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7089998)** — A reproducible mathematical and computational reconstruction of early quantitative finance.
- **[From Forecast-Centric to Trading-Aware](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7217521)** — Risk-constrained decision processes for financial time-series research.
- **[From Data to Models and Backtesting](https://www.asr-lab.online/publications/from-data-to-models-and-backtesting-5f796f00/)** — The research architecture and reproducibility principles behind ASRQuant.
- **[Quantitative Research Under Real-World Constraints](https://www.asr-lab.online/publications/quantitative-research-under-real-world-constraints-30319c6e/)** — Co-authored work on traceable data, modelling, validation and research engineering.
